Drop the three hero photos here with these exact filenames and the landing
page will automatically switch from the illustration to your photos — no
code changes needed:

- `desktop.jpg` — wide crop, used at lg breakpoint and up (landscape hero photo)
- `tablet.jpg` — used at sm–lg breakpoints (portrait/square crop)
- `mobile.jpg` — used below sm (tighter portrait crop)

Recommended: optimized JPG/WebP, under ~300KB each, roughly matching the
aspect ratios of the containers they'll fill (see `resources/views/public/home.blade.php`).
